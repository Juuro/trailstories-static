Closing out the cyclocross winter with the Goldene Ananas finale, then straight into the marathon season with the Schönbuch Trophy and Bike the Rock. The Sudety MTB Challenge in June is the big one this year - four days of stage racing on the Polish-Czech border, my first time doing something like this since the Transalp. Brezelrace in September rounds it off before the cross season starts again.

Let's see how it goes.
