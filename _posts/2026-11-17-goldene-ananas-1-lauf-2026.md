On Sunday, November 15th, the second race of the Goldene Ananas season took place, after the [Prolog]({% post_url 2026-10-13-goldene-ananas-prolog-2026 %}) four weeks earlier. Same format as always: 45 minutes, guerilla style, location announced a day or two before. This time it was somewhere in the deepest part of the forest, a bit below the TV tower in Stuttgart.

I came in with a bit of a question mark. After the Prolog I had basically two weeks without any real bike riding. So I had no idea what to expect from my legs.

## The track

The lap was relatively big, so long in terms of kilometers, and mostly slow and technical:

- very tight turns around trees, up and down, over roots, partly on really loose ground
- one fast descent with a chicane around a tree
- a slightly-more-than-50-meter gentle uphill on a wide forest path
- one spot where I had to push my bike every single lap
- three tree trunks lying across the track. Two of them were no problem at all, you could even hammer over them with some speed. I passed quite a few people there. 😄

In the first laps the course was not really clear to see, and not only I took a wrong line a couple of times. That cost some time and nerves in the beginning.

It was not quite as much fun as the Prolog, but still a very good course. I always have a lot of fun when it is technical but still rideable. Period.

## The race

I got overtaken a few times and I overtook people a few times. I think it was roughly balanced, maybe I even passed a bit more people than passed me. What surprised me again: after two weeks of almost no cycling I handled the race really well. It was great fun.

At the end I was pretty tired, but not completely destroyed. And that is even though the ride to the start was rushed and I arrived about two minutes late. But somehow I have the feeling that such a proper warm-up ride is not that bad for the performance in the actual race.

## The numbers

Strava says 7 laps, 51:42 moving time, 9 km and 306 hm. Average heart rate was 173 bpm (max 187) with an average power of 160 W. My best 5 minutes were at 195 W, the best 20 minutes at 175 W. That is clearly more than at the Prolog (134 W average, 174 W for 5 minutes, 142 W for 20 minutes) at a very similar heart rate. The course was just different: fewer dismounts, fewer sand pits and a pump track, more pedaling.

The laps on the timed segment (1.24 km, 45 hm), in the order Strava lists them:

| Lap | Time | Avg power | Avg heart rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 7:41 | 150 W | 167 bpm |
| 2 | 7:02 | 169 W | 174 bpm |
| 3 | 6:59 | 171 W | 176 bpm |

The slowest lap is the one with the lowest heart rate, which fits the story of the unclear course in the beginning. Laps 2 to 5 are all within seven seconds of each other.

## The result

The results are out: I finished 20th, one lap down on the winner (51:25 on the clock, 7 laps against 8 for the front group). 38 riders got a time in the men's ranking, so that is right in the middle of the field, and it fits my feeling of passing about as many people as passed me. The winner of the day needed 47:49 for his 8 laps.

With the 14 points from this race and the 15 from the Prolog I'm now 16th of 58 in the overall ranking, with 29 points. The goal for the series is a top 4 finish. Fourth place is currently at 46 points, so 17 points are missing. With three more rounds to go (plus the HPAF Intergalactics, which has its own column in the table), that is possible. 🙂

## What went wrong

On the ride home I got a small bonk. I should have taken more food with me. On top of that I left my water bottle at home, which was not ideal either, but still bearable. Lesson for the next one: pack the bag the evening before, not 20 minutes before leaving.

## What did I learn

- Two weeks off the bike is not the end of the world. The base from the last months is still there.
- Technical but rideable is what I like. I should look for more courses like this.
- Arriving rushed is not a disaster. A short, hard ride to the start is a fine warm-up.
- Bring food and water. Always. Even for a 45 minute race plus the ride there and back.

Now I hope that I stay healthy, take the momentum with me and get back into my normal training. The next round is on December 6th.
